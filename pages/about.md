# About Ketworks

Ketworks is a community project on neural quantum states. It has two parts.

- **Ketbook** is a reference on neural quantum states: the variational principle, the architectures, their optimization, and the models on which they are tested.
- **Ketboard** lists variational energies of neural quantum states reported in the literature, one entry per paper, method and instance. Every entry has an error bar and points to the table or page of the paper where it is printed.

Anyone can contribute. Ketbook pages and Ketboard entries are added and corrected through pull requests on [GitHub](https://github.com/ketworks-org/ketworks), and a maintainer reviews each change before it is published. The [contribution guide](https://github.com/ketworks-org/ketworks/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) describes both. The content and the code are released under the MIT License.

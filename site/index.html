Ketbook.

A community-written reference on neural quantum states: the variational principle, estimators, optimizers, architectures and benchmark problems.

Ketboard.

A community-maintained board of reviewed variational energies for explicitly defined benchmark instances.

Written and curated by researchers in the field. One notation across the book, every claim cited to its paper, every energy on the board traced to a table or line of a versioned arXiv source and checked by a curator before it appears. Anyone can propose a page, a correction or a result.

The book

Fundamentals, architectures and benchmark problems in one notation, with the conventions fixed once and every statement cited. Pages are drafts until a curator has reviewed them.

The board

An instance is one exact problem: Hamiltonian, operator convention, cluster, boundaries, couplings and sector. An entry is the printed energy of a paper, with its arXiv version, table and row.

Curation

Curators check eligibility and transcription against the paper before anything is published. Extrapolations, projections and energies quoted from other work are excluded by rule.

    Describe the change and its sources. Automated checks render the LaTeX and validate the data.

    A maintainer reviews the change before merging. The arXiv bot's proposals go through the same review.

Scope of Ketboard

Original, directly evaluated energies of neural variational states. Neural fermionic wavefunctions and neural backflow qualify. Extrapolated energies, projected estimates, non-NQS results and comparison numbers from earlier papers are excluded.

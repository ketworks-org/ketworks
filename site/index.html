Ketbook / Ketboard

Neural quantum states.

Ketbook is a community-written reference on neural quantum states: the variational principle, the estimators, the optimizers, the architectures and the benchmark problems. Ketboard is its community-maintained board of reviewed variational energies for explicitly defined benchmark instances.

Anyone can contribute. Pages are Markdown with LaTeX and results are small data files; every change, text or number, is proposed as a pull request and reviewed by maintainers before it is published.

Scope of Ketboard

Original, directly evaluated energies of neural variational states. Neural fermionic wavefunctions and neural backflow qualify. Extrapolated energies, projected estimates, non-NQS results and comparison numbers from earlier papers are excluded.

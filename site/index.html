02 / Data & evidence

Add a result ↗

Prepare a structured entry with the exact instance, paper version, energy, uncertainty, and source location.

  1. 1

    Make the change.

    Edit a wiki page or prepare a result. Contributors can use their own fork; direct write access is not needed for a public repository.

  2. 2

    Open a pull request.

    Describe the change and its sources. Automated checks validate the content, render the LaTeX, and check the benchmark data.

  3. 3

    Review, then publish.

    A maintainer reviews the change before merging. The arXiv bot uses the same review process for newly discovered results.

The scope of Ketboard.

Original, directly evaluated energies of neural variational states. Neural fermionic wavefunctions and neural backflow can qualify. Extrapolated energies, projected estimates, non-NQS results, and comparison numbers from earlier papers are excluded.

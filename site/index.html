A practical introduction

Learn neural quantum states.

From a parameterized wavefunction to a variational calculation. These short guides introduce the pieces behind the benchmarks.

01 / REPRESENTATION

A wavefunction as a network

A neural quantum state represents amplitudes Ψθ(σ) in a chosen basis. The architecture encodes an inductive bias; its parameters are adjusted to describe a quantum state.

Amplitude and phase, symmetry, and the choice of basis all matter. For fermions, the representation must respect antisymmetry.

Read the foundational paper ↗
02 / MEASUREMENT

Estimate the energy

Eθ = ⟨Ψθ|H|Ψθ⟩ / ⟨Ψθ|Ψθ⟩

Variational Monte Carlo estimates this expectation using samples from |Ψθ|². Its statistical uncertainty is distinct from the physical energy variance and from variation across training runs.

The exact expectation obeys the variational bound. A finite-sample estimate can fluctuate below it.

03 / OPTIMIZATION

Improve the state

Energy gradients and stochastic reconfiguration guide the search over network parameters. Optimization quality depends on sampling, conditioning, and the architecture.

After training, a separate measurement of the fixed state helps make the energy estimate interpretable. Report how samples, autocorrelation, and uncertainty were handled.

04 / COMPARISON

Define the same problem

A useful comparison fixes the Hamiltonian convention, lattice, boundary conditions, couplings, and symmetry or particle-number sector.

Total and per-site energies can be converted when the site count is known. Different operators, offsets, geometries, and extrapolated limits require separate treatment.

Community curation

Make every number traceable.

The collection accepts original variational energies of neural quantum states. A human review is required before publication.

  1. 01

    Discover

    The bot checks arXiv for new papers and revisions. It reads the full text and proposes eligible numerical results with source locations.

  2. 02

    Review the pull request

    Check that the state is an NQS, the estimate is variational, and the physical instance matches exactly. Verify the printed digits, normalization, and meaning of the uncertainty.

  3. 03

    Merge and publish

    Approved results enter the public scoreboard after merge. Unmatched systems remain in proposal files until a curator adds a precise benchmark definition and reviews the matching results.

What belongs in the collection?

Direct energy evaluations of explicit neural variational states, including neural fermionic wavefunctions and neural backflow. A neural guide or sampler alone does not qualify a projected result.

Extrapolated energies, projected estimates, non-NQS methods, and numbers quoted from other papers are excluded. A result does not need to set a record to be included.

Each entry records its exact paper version, source location, original energy and uncertainty, method, and benchmark. Corrections follow the same review process.
